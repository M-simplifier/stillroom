One continuous journey

Stay aboard.
Or follow your curiosity.

01

Watch the world go by

The railcar sets off gently, crossing meadows, woodland, heath, wetlands and dunes. Pause or change the pace whenever you like.

02

Step outside

Stop near dry ground and wander on foot. Walk for yourself, or let automatic walking find a way around trees and water. Your railcar waits where you left it.

03

Pick up where you paused

Esc brings you straight back to work. Your place is saved for the next break. Choose a new landscape only when you feel like a different journey.

Still a listening room

The music stays.
The screen settles.

Four original scores play straight away. Or bring a folder of your own music. Gentle transitions keep it flowing while the controls slip out of view.

Keep a still background for work. For a quieter pause, choose from eleven scenes of water, trees, light and sky. There is no timer to watch or destination to reach.

Explore the short guide →

Made as you go

Terrain, plants, water and the railway are generated locally. The journey grows around you from a saved seed, with a bounded amount of scenery kept in memory.

At home on your computer

No account, network requests or analytics in the app. Your music and settings stay local. Fullscreen on a spare display, or in a small window.

Small, open, yours

Written in Haskell with raylib and native shaders. English and Japanese controls. MIT licensed, with source and a Windows build ready to explore.

Whenever you need a pause

Open a little room.

Extract the ZIP and open stillroom.exe.
Choose Settings → Rest → Journey, then “Continue the journey.”

Download for Windows ↓

An early, unsigned Windows x64 release. A capable graphics device is recommended for the journey.

A few keys, when you want them

R Enter a break · Esc Return to work

Space Stop / ride · E Step outside / board

WASD Walk · Space Automatic walking on foot

Click to look around; click again or press Tab to release. F1 shows the controls.
