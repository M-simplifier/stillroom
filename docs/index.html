Windows x64 · Local playback · Free and open source

Sound without a search

Four procedural scores are built in. Or choose a folder of your own music. Shuffle and gentle transitions keep it playing.

Room for your attention

A static green, slate, black or lake view. Fullscreen on a spare display, or a small window. No feed, account or notifications.

Small by design

A pure Haskell core with a thin Windows boundary. MIT source, local settings, and no network requests. English and Japanese controls.

Extract. Open. Settle in.

Open stillroom.exe from the extracted ZIP. Press Space to pause, N for the next track, and Esc for a small window. Move the mouse to reveal the controls.
