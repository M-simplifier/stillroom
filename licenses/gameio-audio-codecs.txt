GameIO audio codec source attribution

Ogg encapsulation follows RFC 3533.
https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3533

Vorbis follows the Xiph.Org Vorbis I specification. The setup, floor,
residue and channel routing implementations also consulted the MIT
jfreymuth/vorbis implementation at
8e67b20ce3e838bb7a92123fe7992a25b9fcdbfe.
GameIO's inverse transform uses a separately implemented radix-2 FFT
and DCT-IV reduction.
https://xiph.org/vorbis/doc/Vorbis_I_spec.html
https://github.com/jfreymuth/vorbis/tree/8e67b20ce3e838bb7a92123fe7992a25b9fcdbfe

The Layer III Huffman trees and synthesis window in Mp3Tables.hs are
transcribed from Herschel/puremp3 at eeb913d6f61cd3ae77aa4d8cb38c6cb3f0f58602.
That project lists Mike Welsh as author and licenses its code under MIT or
CC0. This distribution preserves its MIT notice below. GameIO computes the
IMDCT and synthesis cosine tables from the transform equations.
https://github.com/Herschel/puremp3/tree/eeb913d6f61cd3ae77aa4d8cb38c6cb3f0f58602

The Layer III scale-factor band widths were transcribed from lieff/minimp3
under CC0 1.0. The reference minimp3.h had SHA-256
57e437c5c1f0e8b243885d3929c8973b5e6c778451e0100ab4251d19915cb3ad.
Its scalefactor grouping and intensity-stereo rules were also consulted.
https://github.com/lieff/minimp3
