# Built-in audio attribution

The modified piano samples in `assets/audio/music/felt-*.wav` are derived from
**Salamander Grand Piano v3 by Alexander Holm**, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
The FLAC distribution used here was remapped by **kinwie**. Original project:
[Salamander Grand Piano v3](https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3).

Stillroom obtained the source recordings from
[sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano, commit 3382bf9496bba2486f5ab0de55a264d1dfc38404](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano/tree/3382bf9496bba2486f5ab0de55a264d1dfc38404).
The upstream [README attribution](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano/blob/3382bf9496bba2486f5ab0de55a264d1dfc38404/README.md)
and [license](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano/blob/3382bf9496bba2486f5ab0de55a264d1dfc38404/LICENSE)
are retained by reference; the complete license is copied in
[Salamander-Grand-Piano-CC-BY-3.0.txt](Salamander-Grand-Piano-CC-BY-3.0.txt).

**Modifications:** Stillroom selects the soft velocity layer, trims the recordings,
changes pitch and sample rate, filters and adjusts gain, adds shaped attack/release
and an original synthesized room tail, then exports stereo 44.1 kHz PCM16 WAVs.
These modified piano samples are distributed under CC BY 3.0, with this attribution
and modification notice. No endorsement by Alexander Holm or kinwie is implied.
The SFZ mapping itself is not incorporated into Stillroom.

All other bundled music one-shots and environmental sounds are original seeded
synthesis generated by the MIT-licensed scripts in `tools/audio/`; those sounds
are offered under Stillroom's [MIT license](../LICENSE). The code, compositions
and original synthesized room response are also MIT. This does not relicense the
underlying Salamander recordings as MIT.

The bank manifests and `tools/audio/piano-sources.json` retain filenames, SHA-256
identities and the pinned public source. Reproduction instructions are in
[tools/audio/README.md](../tools/audio/README.md). The application uses bundled
WAVs and does not fetch samples at startup.
